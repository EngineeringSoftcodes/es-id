Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my ES ID to Steam?

Go to the Linked Accounts section in your dashboard and select "Connect Steam". Follow the prompts to authorize your Steam account.

Can I use ES ID on console platforms like Xbox or PlayStation?

Yes! ES ID supports linking Xbox and PlayStation accounts for cross-platform gameplay and syncing achievements.

How secure is ES ID?

We use end-to-end encryption, 2FA, and secure hashing to protect user data across all connected platforms and services.

How can I request access to the API or source code?

To request access, please contact us with a valid reason. Source code access has a one-time fee of €50 and requires approval.

What should I do if I forgot my password?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen. You’ll receive an email with reset instructions.
