Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my ES ID to a platform (like Steam, Epic Games, etc)?

1. Log into your ES ID account, go to your Dashboard, then click on Settings → Account Connections.

2. There you will see icons or names of different platforms (e.g., Steam, Epic Games, Discord). Click on the one you want to connect.

3. A new window will open. Log in to your chosen account using your username/email and password, and confirm the connection.

4. Once connected, it will say "Connected". If you don't see the platform listed or experience any issues, please contact ES Support.

How secure is ES ID?

We use end-to-end encryption, Two-Factor Authentication (2FA), and secure password hashing to protect your data across all platforms.

What should I do if I forgot my password?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. You’ll receive an email with reset instructions. If you don’t get the email or need help, please contact ES Support.

How ES ID Works

ES ID is a secure account and identity system developed by Engineering Softcodes™. It lets players and users log in across games and apps with a single ID — powered by fast and modern API tech.

Partner Access

To use ES ID in your own project, you must be an official partner. After approval, you’ll receive access to our API keys, developer documentation, and optional source code.
